Engagement Ring Metallurgy

Precious Metals for Your Forever Ring

950 Pure Platinum

The apex metal for diamond rings. Naturally hypoallergenic, ultra-dense, and never yellows. It secures diamonds with unparalleled lifetime tensile strength.

18k Royal Yellow Gold

Rich, regal warmth with 75% pure gold alloyed with copper and silver for ring durability. Complements warm skin tones and vintage settings beautifully.

18k Luminous White Gold

Pure gold fused with palladium and plated with premium rhodium for an icy mirror shine that illuminates white diamond facets.

18k Romantic Rose Gold

Delicate blush glow created with refined copper alloy. Romantic, modern, and striking when contrasted against ice-white diamond halos.
