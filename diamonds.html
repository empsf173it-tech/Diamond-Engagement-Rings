Popular Engagement Ring Diamond Shapes

Round Brilliant

The quintessential diamond cut engineered mathematically for supreme light reflection and scintillation.

Oval Cut

Elongating & Flattering

A contemporary favorite combining the sparkle of a round cut with a graceful elongated profile on the finger.

Emerald Cut

Step Cut • Hall of Mirrors

Sophisticated rectangular open table facets exhibiting pristine vintage glamour and mirror-flash reflection.

Cushion Cut

Romantic Pillow Edges

Square or rectangular with soft rounded corners and deep facets that evoke vintage candlelit radiance.

Pear Cut

Teardrop Silhouette

Dramatic blend of round and marquise cuts, tapering to a sharp point for unforgettable avant-garde presence.

Radiant Cut

Marquise Cut

Commissioned by King Louis XV of France, offering the largest surface area per carat of any diamond shape.

Gemological Science

Mastering the 4 Cs for Engagement Rings

Speak to a GIA Gemologist

1. CUT — Optical Performance & Polish

The Prime Determinant of Sparkle

A diamond's cut grade doesn't refer to its shape, but how skillfully its facets interact with light. Celestia accepts solely Triple Excellent (Cut, Polish, and Symmetry) grades, preventing dull "leakage" so light is reflected back to your eyes as pure rainbow scintillation.

Celestia Standard: Only GIA 'Excellent' or AGS 'Ideal 0' cut grades are permitted in our engagement rings.

2. COLOR — Purity & Absence of Tone

Graded on D-to-Z Scale

The less color a white diamond contains, the rarer and more valuable it is. The global standard begins at D (Colorless). Our atelier focuses strictly on the D, E, F, and G range, guaranteeing that in platinum or white gold, your ring sparkles with icy crystalline perfection.

Celestia Standard: D-F (Colorless) for Platinum settings; G-H (Near Colorless) for 18k Yellow Gold settings.

3. CLARITY — Purity Under 10x Magnification

Flawless to Eye-Clean

During deep mantle crystal formation, internal characteristics (inclusions) and external blemishes occur. We hand-inspect every diamond to verify that it is 100% Eye-Clean, ensuring that no spots, feathers, or clouds will ever interrupt your ring's transparency.

Celestia Standard: FL (Flawless), IF (Internally Flawless), VVS1-VVS2, and selected eye-clean VS1-VS2 stones.

4. CARAT — Physical Weight & Millimeter Spread

1 Carat = 200 Milligrams

Carat measures physical weight, but table spread determines how large the diamond appears from above. Our master cutters balance carat weight with optimal depth percentage to deliver the largest visual presence without losing edge light.

Celestia Standard: We source bespoke single stones from 1.00ct through 10.00ct+ with full laser inscription on the girdle.
