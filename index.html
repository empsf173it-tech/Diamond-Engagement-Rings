Diamond Mastery

The Celestia 4 Cs Diamond Standard

Every diamond selected for Celestia surpasses global industry baselines. We source only the top 1% of gem-quality diamonds in the world.

Complete Diamond Education

1. CUT

Triple Excellent

The single most vital factor determining fire, brilliance, and scintillation. We curate only ideal and excellent proportions.

2. COLOR

D to G Colorless

Graded in ice-white pure tones. Free of visible yellow or brown undertones, reflecting pure radiant daylight sparkle.

3. CLARITY

FL to VS1 Eye-Clean

Every diamond is certified eye-clean by master gemologists. Completely devoid of inclusions visible to the naked eye.

4. CARAT

1.00ct to 10.00ct+

Precision weight cut for maximum millimeter spread. Get fuller surface visibility without sacrificing depth or optical fire.
